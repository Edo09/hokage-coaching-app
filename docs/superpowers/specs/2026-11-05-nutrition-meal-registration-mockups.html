1 · The day

Status on the right of every meal: a red ✓ for a meal from the plan, a grey ⇄ for «otra comida», a red outline for the next meal and a dashed box for the meals still to come. Registered meals show their photo on the left and the kcal before the check. The next meal is open with its options.

2 · Registering a plan option

The option shown on the card comes pre-selected. The photo is optional. «Guardar» saves at once, and the AI estimate follows on the server. «No la comí», the last and quietest choice, hides the photo row; optional meals don't offer it.

3 · «Comí otra cosa»

The text stays exactly as typed until the client saves. After saving, the AI fixes only spelling, accents and capital letters, and estimates the meal. The photo is optional.

5 · Later: a skipped meal

«No la comí» counts the merienda as done («4 de 5») with no kcal, photo or AI: a muted card with a grey «–» box. In the panel it reads «No comió». The almuerzo's estimate has landed with only its spelling fixed («arros» → «Arroz», «abichuelas» → «habichuelas»).
